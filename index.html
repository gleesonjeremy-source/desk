❓ How this works — read me once

What this page is: the human window on the trading machine. It answers two questions: what needs me? and where is the project? Everything here is read-only and machine-written — nothing on this page is ever hand-edited.

🎯 Needs you — your action list. P1 = something running wrong → repair first (tell Claude tonight: "run the drill"). Pad items = ideas you dropped (desk: idea from Telegram) waiting to be absorbed in an evening session. Bench decisions + dreamer items = calls only you can make. Green line = machine's fine without you today.

🏁 Goals & proving ground — the ladder every strategy climbs before real money: 🌱 collecting → ✅ paper-proven → 🏦 TS-SIM → 💵 live-small. Version-aware: strategies evolve through numbered rulesets (v1, v2, v3…) and the verdict grades the CURRENT version's own trades — a redesigned strategy starts a fresh 🌱 cohort instead of wearing its dead ancestor's grade. The ↳ line under each bar is the lineage: what the retired versions did and why they died. Bars show verified reps toward the 20-rep verdict. This is "is the machine earning trust", not account value — your real numbers live in the option tracker.

🔍 Today's signals — what the scanners found this morning (top of the ranked digest). The phone pitches are the curated subset; this is the fuller board.

💼 The pitches — did they pay? — the money funnel: what the machine pitched to your phone → which ones YOU took for real → what happened. This is the panel that answers "is the phone channel making me money." The habit that powers it: when you take a pitch for real, tell the tracker session "<TICKER> found by scanner" — that stamps the link, and your personal take-rate and hit-rate start accumulating. Paper outcomes shown here; real dollars stay in the tracker.

🎯 Are the ideas good? — the machine grading its own picks. Every HIGH/MED idea automatically runs as a paper trade; this panel shows what those trades actually did, split by conviction tier and by feed (pitched to your phone vs board-only). The contract with yourself: HIGH must beat MEDIUM — if it doesn't, the panel flags it and the ranking gets fixed. This is how "the ideas must improve over time" stops being a hope and becomes a number.

🏟️ The traders' book — the tournament agents' open trades: exact contract, live P&L, and their plan (thesis + target/stop). This is where you coach: spot a mistake or a better idea, text the bot desk: tell vega the TLT put is fighting the Fed, size down — it lands on the Pad and gets absorbed into their guidance next session. Their money is paper; your judgment is the edge they're missing.

🗓️ The road to the goals — the dated waypoints between here and an autonomous live account (each tagged with the goal it feeds). Countdown turns amber inside two weeks, red when overdue. Every build should feed a milestone — if something on "now building" doesn't, ask why. You steer from your phone: desk: bump <thing> reorders priorities; Claude does the legwork and the queue reshuffles by the next pass.

🗺️ The build — now building (max a few), next up, and what actually shipped recently. If "now building" ever exceeds ~2 items, tell Claude to park one.

⏰ The clock — every scheduled job the project runs: what fires when (Pacific) and what it does. The mini rows are read live from launchd on every pass, so this panel can't drift from what's actually installed — 🔴 means a job exists but isn't loaded (a repair). The "off the mini" rows are schedulers on other machines (like Claude-app tasks on the laptop), kept by hand. If a job appears here you don't recognize, ask Claude — nothing should run that this panel doesn't explain.

📈 Trades closed per week — the project's speedometer. Every strategy must finish ~20 practice (paper) trades before it's trusted with real money — so the faster trades close, the faster a strategy gets its verdict: proven (moves toward your real account) or killed (stops wasting time). Each bar = one week, labeled by that week's Monday; the number is how many practice trades finished that week. Healthy bars = the machine is learning. Shrinking bars = it's idling — ask Claude why.

Freshness: data refreshes ~hourly on market days (evening passes daily); the stamp at the top tells you its age, and an amber banner appears if it's stale. Pull-to-refresh / reload any time; the page also re-fetches every 5 minutes.

The key: this page reads the same locked database as the tracker. One key per device, asked once, kept in the browser (Telegram bot command help if you forget everything else). Depth lives in Obsidian — this page is the surface.
