What this page is: the human window on the trading machine, ordered by your morning hour. Everything here is read-only and machine-written; nothing is ever hand-edited. Depth lives in Obsidian; real dollars live in the tracker.

🎯 IDEAS — the window (Telegram is only the bell): your book's counts first, then the tickets with chart grade + record label and ONE 📋 COPY TICKET button each, held names, radar, study card, and "N more on the board" folded underneath.

🫵 TODAY — everything that needs you, in one list: alarms (a job down, suspect marks, tracker drift, a P1), dated asks for the day, and the 🗳️ decisions. A decision reads in plain words with the machine's rec. 📲 rows are answered in Telegram: reply the number and your pick ("3 park") or "all recs"; silent past the date = the rec is taken for you (shown for 7 days, "3 no — why" reverses it). 🛠️ rows wait for the next Mac Pro session and are never auto-taken. Machine fixes never ask you — they queue as the batch.

📰 What's going on — the week's stories: an agent's first of a strategy, a trade gaining momentum, an agent heating up, a strategy working (or gone cold) right now, sim and paper closes, verdict changes, ships, hot tickers.

📆 The calendar — the ONE place for dates: the next 21 days of builds, your asks, rituals, expiries, earnings, macro; landed-this-week, in-flight, slipped and the waiting line fold inside it.

🖥️ State of the machine — the four buckets: ✅ WORKING · 🔧 DEVELOPING · 🌱 DISCOVERED (needs reps) · 🛠️ NEEDS WORK. Rules over the registry, verdicts, rep-rate, TS-SIM and the scan series; version chips per strategy; any verdict/mode mismatch shows red and rings Sunday. The goal lights and rent meter sit in its header.

Folded reference: 🏦 TS-SIM (real limit orders, real fills, zero dollars), 🏟️ the tournament (leader · drama · worth watching, standings + the open book inside), ✅ the ladder (🌱 collecting → ✅ paper-proven → 🏦 TS-SIM → 💵 live-small) with the weekly rep bars, and 📖 the playbook (how to drive the machine from your phone).

Freshness: data refreshes ~hourly on market days and every morning + evening including weekends; the stamp at the top tells its age, an amber banner appears past ~14h. The page re-fetches every 5 minutes.

The key: this page reads the same locked database as the tracker. One key per device, asked once, kept in the browser (Telegram bot command help if you forget everything else).
