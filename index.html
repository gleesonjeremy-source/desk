💼 YOUR ACCOUNT — first, always: the score (account value, growth, closed P&L vs the floor), 🎓 what's closest to real money (the sim exam), 🩹 the one-word verdict on every real position (ROLL · HOLD · BUY BACK · LET GO · HARVEST · NO CALL — tap for the why), 💳 room against YOUR 50% margin ceiling (red past it), 📒 your book's balance, one machine light.

💡 What's new — discoveries first (heating up, working or gone cold, firsts, verdicts, hot tickers), then the notable rest; five lines, the week on a tap.
