❓ How this works — read me once

What this page is: the human window on the trading machine — the whole project in one scroll. Everything here is read-only and machine-written; nothing is ever hand-edited. Depth lives in Obsidian; real dollars live in the tracker.

⏳ The strip (top) — the front page: 📰 top of the news (the machine watches the whole project and reports the day's biggest story — ships, verdicts, hot traders, hot tickers, milestone hits; "more news" fold underneath), 🔨 next build, 📈 the proof line (the strongest current evidence we're getting closer to the goals), milestone chips (event-driven milestones show "x of N closes · pace ETA" — they fire on their condition, dates are only review checkpoints), and the one-line machine health check (🧹 vault · ⏰ jobs · 🚚 deploys verifying · 💭 dreamer · 📥 Pad).

🎯 Needs you — your action list. P1 = something running wrong → repair first. Pad items = ideas you dropped (desk: idea from Telegram) waiting to be absorbed. Bench decisions + dreamer items = calls only you can make. Green line = machine's fine without you today.

💡 Best trades today — the top of the ranked board from this morning's scans: exact contract, why, and the risk numbers. The phone pitches are the curated cut; this is the fuller board. Prices are as-of scan time — always verify the live mid before entering.

🏦 TS-SIM — the broker truth — the machine's trades run as REAL limit orders in the TradeStation simulator: real fills, real no-fills, zero dollars. The headline is the fill-truth gap: what the broker actually paid vs what paper mid-marks claimed. No strategy touches real money without passing here.

🏟️ The tournament — the five-agent paper league, built to teach how to trade. The headline is the true 👑 (ranked on merit — the engine's own basis: equity minus relegation tax, suspect marks excluded — so tax recycling can't fake first place) plus the relegation drama. ⚡ Worth watching is the radar: a trader on fire (with their newest trade, copyable, flagged if still enterable), first defenses, fresh opens/closes with the why, experiments, confluence. Full standings and every agent's open book fold underneath. Coach them: text desk: tell vega … — it lands on the Pad and shapes their next session.

✅ What's working — the whole evidence story in one card: the goals, the proving-ground ladder every strategy climbs (🌱 collecting → ✅ paper-proven → 🏦 TS-SIM → 💵 live-small, version-aware — a redesigned strategy starts a fresh cohort), the weekly rep speedometer, and folded inside: the machine grading its own picks (HIGH must beat MEDIUM) and the pitch funnel (what it pitched → what you took → did it pay; stamp takes with "<TICKER> found by scanner" in a tracker session).

🔮 The dreamer — the overnight suggestion engine's ledger: what's pending your look, and the proof its ideas get handled (each handled item shows its resolution). If pending climbs and handled doesn't, the dreamer is being ignored — say "absorb dreamer" in a session.

🔬 The bench — the research state: foundry candidates (hypotheses earning a registry entry, each with its stage and PASS/FAIL gate) and the measured-validation shelf (what's been backtested and what it showed). Request work any time: desk: backtest <idea> — it lands on the Pad and enters the queue.

📅 The week — what's scheduled: this week's dated builds (P1s red), next week folded, and anything that slipped its date. This is the smart-calendar v1; earnings dates and the verdict docket join at the next build.

🗓️ The road — every dated waypoint between here and an autonomous live account, each tagged with the goal it feeds. You steer from your phone: desk: bump <thing> reorders priorities.

🗺️ The build — now building, next up, the deploy dock, and what shipped. If "now building" exceeds ~2 items, tell Claude to park one.

⏰ The clock — every scheduled job: what fires when (Pacific), read live from launchd so it can't drift. 🔴 = installed but not loaded — a repair. If a job appears here you don't recognize, ask Claude.

Freshness: data refreshes ~hourly on market days, and every morning at 7:00 + evenings even on weekends/holidays (the 08-15 repair — Saturday shows Saturday); the stamp at the top tells you its age, and an amber banner appears past ~14h. The page re-fetches every 5 minutes.

The key: this page reads the same locked database as the tracker. One key per device, asked once, kept in the browser (Telegram bot command help if you forget everything else).
